## Why not "pantalonetas"

The owner's draft used "pantalonetas" for leggings. Research (2026-10-04) says:

- In much of Latin America (Colombia, Central America) **pantaloneta means athletic shorts**,
  not leggings, so a shopper searching "pantalonetas" expects shorts.
- In Peru, leggings are **calzas** (or "leggings"), used interchangeably; sports retailers sell
  "calzas deportivas".
- In the BJJ trade (English) the compression leggings are **spats**; shorter versions are
  "spats shorts" or vale tudo shorts; many athletes wear spats and grappling shorts together.

So the site says **Shorts**, **Shorts de compresión** and **Leggings** (with "calzas de
compresión" in the descriptive copy). If your customers in Lima really say "pantalonetas" for
leggings, add it as a secondary word in the body copy of `shorts.html`, not in the
page title or navigation.

Not verified: how Lima academies themselves say it. Ask two or three coaches and adjust.
