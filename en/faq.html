FAQ

What people usually ask us.

If you can’t find your answer, message us on WhatsApp.

What do you make?

Rashguards (short and long sleeve), fight shorts, compression shorts and spats (leggings), customized for academies and brands. See the rashguards and the shorts and spats.

Can I use my own design?

Yes. Bring your design or build a new one with us, with details such as side mesh panels. If you already have files, share a link in the quote form or on WhatsApp.

How does an order work?

Five steps with one point of contact: scope, design, production, quality and delivery. See the full process →

When is the price agreed?

Before production. Quantities, specs, deadlines and budget are in writing before we start.

Will a reorder match the first order?

That is the standard for every order: each piece is checked before it ships and the second order matches the first.

Do you make kids’ apparel?

Yes. We have made rashguards for academies’ kids, as well as adult ones.

What is the minimum order?

It depends on the product and the design. Message us on WhatsApp with what you need and we confirm it in writing before we start.

How long does an order take?

It depends on the product, the quantity and the design. The date is agreed in writing before we start: a date given is a date kept.

Can I get a sample first?

Tell us on WhatsApp what you need and we confirm how we handle it in your case.

How do I set sizes?

Sizes are set with your order, before production. Message us and we walk you through it.

Do you ship to the United States?

We work with academies in Lima and Miami. We confirm shipping terms for your case in writing before we start.

How do I pay?

Payment terms are agreed in writing together with the budget, before production.

Have another question?

One order. Total execution. Made in Peru.
